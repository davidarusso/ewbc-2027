## Welcome to EWBC 2027

The 13<sup>th</sup> European Workshop on the Molecular Biology of Cyanobacteria will be held in Leipzig, Germany, from September 14<sup>th</sup> to 17<sup>th</sup>, 2027. 

Building on a long-standing tradition of the EWBC, the Local Organizing Committee and International Scientific Committee are excited to present a program spanning the frontiers of cyanobacterial research and beyond. We warmly invite you to join us in Germany for inspiring talks, vibrant networking opportunities, and an inspiring scientific exchange.

### Important Dates
* **Abstract Submission Opens:** Month Day, 2026
* **Abstract Deadline:** Month Day, 2027
* **Conference Dates:** Month Day–Day, 2027
